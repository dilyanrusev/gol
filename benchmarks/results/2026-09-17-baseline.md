# Baseline, 2026-09-17

Before any performance work. Compare later runs against these tables; allocations are exact and
portable, timings are only comparable on the same machine and configuration.

```
BenchmarkDotNet v0.15.2, Windows 11 (10.0.26200.9457)
.NET SDK 10.0.300, .NET 10.0.12 (10.0.1226.42308), X64 RyuJIT AVX2
Workstation GC, Release, IterationCount=8, WarmupCount=2
```

Worlds: `gun@1000` is Gosper's glider gun after 1000 generations (about 200 cells), `acorn@5000`
the acorn after 5000 generations (about 800 cells), `soup50k` 50 000 random cells in a 500 × 500
square (seed 12345).

## Engine: one generation, one snapshot copy

| Method   | World      | Mean         | Allocated |
|----------|------------|-------------:|----------:|
| Step     | gun@1000   |   106.6 μs   |         - |
| Snapshot | gun@1000   |     3.7 μs   |   3 432 B |
| Step     | acorn@5000 |   109.4 μs   |         - |
| Snapshot | acorn@5000 |    13.1 μs   |  12 888 B |
| Step     | soup50k    | 9 576.1 μs   |         - |
| Snapshot | soup50k    |    86.7 μs   | 800 024 B |

Stepping allocates nothing on any world: the neighbour-count dictionary and the two cell sets are
reused. A snapshot costs exactly 16 bytes per live cell (one `Cell` of two `ulong`s) plus the
array header, and is taken once per generation.

## Viewport projection (per client, per generation), 50 000-cell soup

| Method  | Size | Mean       | Gen0  | Gen1  | Gen2  | Allocated |
|---------|-----:|-----------:|------:|------:|------:|----------:|
| Project |  100 |   173.2 μs |   2.0 |     - |     - |  24.11 KB |
| Project |  500 | 2 366.8 μs | 171.9 | 164.1 | 164.1 | 707.75 KB |

Projection walks the whole population for every client, so its time is O(population) even for a
small viewport. The 500 × 500 case allocates on the large object heap (Gen2 collections): the
growing `List<int>`, its sort, and the final array.

## Frame on the wire (JSON as SignalR sends it), soup

| Method        | Size | Mean      | Gen0 | Gen1 | Gen2 | Allocated |
|---------------|-----:|----------:|-----:|-----:|-----:|----------:|
| SerializeJson |  100 |  10.5 μs  |  0.8 |    - |    - |  10.08 KB |
| SerializeJson |  500 | 309.6 μs  | 90.8 | 90.8 | 90.8 | 321.36 KB |

Each packed index is serialised as decimal text; a dense 500 × 500 view is a 300 KB message per
client per generation.

## Whole server tick (step + snapshot + per-client projection and JSON), 100 × 100 viewports

Eight invocations per iteration (`invocationCount: 8`), because single-invocation measurements
of the soup tick varied by ±80 %.

| Method | World      | Clients | Mean         | Allocated   |
|--------|------------|--------:|-------------:|------------:|
| Tick   | acorn@5000 |       1 |    193.7 μs  |    20.42 KB |
| Tick   | acorn@5000 |       4 |    329.6 μs  |    40.06 KB |
| Tick   | acorn@5000 |      16 |    749.7 μs  |    62.11 KB |
| Tick   | soup50k    |       1 | 12 946.2 μs  |   716.12 KB |
| Tick   | soup50k    |       4 | 13 374.0 μs  |   817.18 KB |
| Tick   | soup50k    |      16 | 15 783.2 μs  | 1 213.19 KB |

Reading: at 60 generations per second the budget per tick is 16.7 ms. The soup already needs
13 ms for the step alone, so the ceiling is the engine, not the clients; each extra 100 × 100
client adds roughly 35 μs and 30 KB on the acorn, mostly the JSON text. On small worlds the
per-client cost is dominated by projection walking the whole population.

## What the budgets in the Core tests guard

- Stepping a steady population allocates nothing (acorn after it settles, the pulsar, the soup).
  A growing population, such as the gun, legitimately makes the engine's sets double their capacity
  now and then, so it is not held to that.
- A snapshot allocates 16 bytes per cell plus the array header.
- Projecting the soup allocates at most 32 KB at 100 × 100 and 760 KB at 500 × 500.
